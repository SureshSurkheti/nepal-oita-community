About Nepal-Oita Community

Learn about our mission, history, and the activities we organize to connect Nepali people living in Oita.

Our Mission

Nepal-Oita Community aims to provide a friendly platform for Nepali residents in Oita to connect, share culture, support each other, and enjoy social events together.

Our History

The community started in 2018 when a few Nepali friends in Oita wanted to create a space for cultural exchange and mutual support. Since then, we have grown to a vibrant group hosting events, festivals, and educational workshops.

Our Activities

  • Cultural events and festivals
  • Language and educational workshops
  • Community gatherings and networking
  • Support for Nepali residents in Oita

# Photograph credits

**The site no longer shows these.** The credit strips were removed from the
footer, the gallery and the lightbox on request. This file is what is left of
that information, so it is not lost — and so the decision below can be revisited
without anybody having to work out who took what all over again.

## Read this before publishing

Six of the photographs checked into `public/images/` are Creative Commons
licensed, and **attribution is a condition of those licences, not a courtesy.**
Publishing them with no credit anywhere is a breach of the licence — the same
category of problem as using a stock photo without paying for it, not a matter of
taste.

Two ways to make the live site legitimate, either of which is fine:

1. **Replace those six files** with photographs the community took itself. Then
   nothing on the site needs a credit and this file can go. This is the better
   answer anyway: they are placeholder scenery, and real photographs of Oita and
   of Nepal taken by members would say more.
2. **Put one line back.** A single link in the footer to a credits page satisfies
   CC BY and CC BY-SA; it does not have to appear on every page, which is what
   the strips were doing. Ask and it is a ten-minute change.

Gallery photographs are a separate matter: their credits are stored per row in
the database and are still recorded by the committee when a photo is uploaded
(`/admin/photos`). Those fields were deliberately kept, so the information keeps
accruing even while nothing displays it.
